Night trains to get sleeper coaches with ramps from January

Pathik Rail will add 120 accessible sleeper coaches to its night trains from 15 January 2027. Each coach will have a ramp at the door, two wider lower berths and Braille numbering on every berth, the operator said on Saturday.

The first routes are Chennai–Bengaluru, Mumbai–Pune and Howrah–Patna. Passengers who use a wheelchair can book the wider berths in the "Divyang" quota, which opens 60 days before the journey.

Disability groups welcomed the move but asked for audio announcements inside coaches. "A blind passenger still cannot tell which station is next," said Meera Iyer of the fictional Travel For All Trust.

The operator said announcements in eleven languages would follow later in 2027.

Pathik Samachar and Pathik Rail are fictional, made for testing the Drishti accessibility project.
